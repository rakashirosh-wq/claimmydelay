What to do next

  1. Find the airline's official claims/customer-relations email or web form — we link to most major airlines' claims pages from our airline directory.
  2. Send the letter as-is, or paste it into their web form if they don't accept email.
  3. Airlines are generally expected to respond within a few weeks — if you hear nothing after 4-6 weeks, it's worth following up or escalating to your country's national enforcement body.
  4. If the airline denies the claim citing "extraordinary circumstances" and you believe that's wrong, that's exactly the situation where a full-service claims company chasing it for you can be worth the commission — see our honest comparison.
